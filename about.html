About our organic colors

We focus on simple, traditional and nature-based pigments. Our goal is to provide Holi colors that feel safer compared to harsh chemical powders.

  • Natural sources: flowers, fruits, leaves, and plant extracts
  • Two types: dry dust and liquid
  • Small-batch approach for freshness and consistent quality
How it helps
  • Less irritation risk (still do a patch test if sensitive)
  • Gentler on hair and skin
  • Better for the environment (less synthetic residue)
